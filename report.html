One hallucination, found and fixed on the record

The first full benchmark run wasn't 0/20 — it was 1/20. The fix attempt that failed is kept in the log next to the one that worked.

Attempt 1 — verified not to work

C03, Trust in others: "I trust people completely… actually no, I don't trust a single person anymore." Scored confidently at 1/5 (0.95 confidence) by reading only the second half of a direct contradiction. An abstract system-prompt rule ("don't resolve contradictions by recency") was added and checked narrowly on this exact case — identical output. The rule alone didn't change the model's behavior.

Attempt 2 — verified working, then re-verified at full scale

Replaced the abstract rule with a concrete worked example in the prompt (wrong answer vs. right answer, spelled out). Re-checked the same case: correct insufficient_evidence, citing the contradiction itself. The full 12-conversation benchmark was then re-run — not just the one facet — to confirm nothing else regressed. Result: 0/20 hallucinations, 18/20 agreement, unchanged.
